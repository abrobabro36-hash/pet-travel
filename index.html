Tips for Traveling with Pets 🐶🐱

📋

Check the Policy

Always confirm pet weight limits directly with the hotel before booking.

💉

Update Vaccinations

Most hotels require vaccination records. Carry copies digitally.

🎒

Pack a Pet Kit

Bring food, bowl, leash, waste bags, and a familiar toy.

✍️

Note at Booking

Always mention your pet (breed + weight) in comments for smooth check-in.

Why use PetTravel Finder?

Traveling with pets can be stressful when finding accommodation. Many hotels have hidden fees or breed restrictions. Our system forces automated filtering to help you find hotels, apartments, and guest houses worldwide that genuinely welcome pets.

How to book a pet-friendly room:

  • Enter your destination city and travel dates in the search bar above.
  • Choose your preferred booking platform and toggle "With Pets" on.
  • Important: Always leave a note during booking stating you travel with animals to ensure a hassle-free check-in.
